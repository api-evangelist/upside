# Upside

> Upside (upside.com) is a consumer cash-back rewards platform connecting retailers with consumers on everyday purchases — fuel, grocery, and restaurant — used by more than 5 million people. For businesses it offers the Upside Marketplace, Measurement, Loyalty+, and partner integrations delivered through a partner-gated, enterprise-ready API for personalized offers; no public developer portal or self-serve API documentation is published.

## Company

- [Website](https://www.upside.com/): Consumer cash-back app for gas, grocery, and dining
- [For Business](https://www.upside.com/business): Upside Marketplace, Partner Integrations (API), Measurement, Loyalty+
- [Tech Integration Partnerships](https://www.upside.com/partnerships/tech-integration): Partner-gated "enterprise-ready API" for personalized, evergreen offers
